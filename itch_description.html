🦎 Chameleon Quest

Swing through sticky situations and tongue-lash your way to victory!

Embark on a vibrant adventure as a chameleon with a knack for acrobatics. Use your sticky tongue to traverse dangerous terrain, grapple across vast gaps, and outsmart your enemies in this fast-paced platformer.

⚠️ WEB DEMO NOTICE: The web version is a LITE DEMO. Features like Login, Leaderboards, and Progress Saving are disabled.

👉 For the full experience, please download the Windows Desktop version below!

🛡️ Note: Windows might warn you about the EXE being unrecognized. This is normal for indie games. Click 'More Info' -> 'Run Anyway'.

🎮 Controls

  • Move: A / D or ← / →
  • Jump: Space or W
  • Grapple Hook: G or Left Mouse Click
  • Shoot Tongue: Right Mouse Click
  • Toggle Fullscreen: F
  • Mute Music: M

✨ Features

  • Dynamic Movement: Master the art of the grapple hook and wall jumps.
  • Combat: Use your chameleon tongue to zap enemies!
  • Multiple Levels: Challenge yourself across different stages.
  • Settings: Customize your experience with fullscreen and audio controls.
